    What is BlueLab?

    The idea behind the project: students act as compliance officers and work through realistic blue-economy cases, starting with marine genetic resources.

  3. 11:45 – 12:30

    How the platform works

    A short, non-technical walk-through of the workflow: what the AI tool does, and where the user checks its output against the legal sources. The workflow is built for teaching, but it is designed to support anyone doing compliance checks, from students to practitioners.

    Rohan Nanda

  6. 14:15 – 15:00

  7. 15:00 – 15:15

    Coffee

  8. 15:15 – 15:45

    Discussion 3: Questions and outputs

    What should students be asked to do? What would a good answer look like? Does the workflow fit how this work is done in practice?

  9. 15:45 – 16:00

    Wrap-up and next steps
