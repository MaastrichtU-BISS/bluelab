BlueLab

AI Due Diligence Lab for the Blue Economy

BlueLab places students in the role of sustainability compliance officers for realistic blue-economy cases. Students use AI responsibly to triage legal and corporate texts, construct obligation registers, and deliver client-ready outputs.

About the project

The project addresses a gap between education and professional practice, where AI tools are already used for document analysis but graduates are not trained to use them responsibly.

Students take on the role of compliance officers and work through realistic cases from fragmented regulatory texts in a problem-based learning setting.

The project will deliver a lightweight student-facing dashboard where students analyse curated case materials and record prompts and outputs for assessment and reflection. Built-in guardrails flag unverified AI outputs for human-in-the-loop verification, and all steps are logged to maintain a full provenance trail.

Innovation

A practice-oriented workflow, not just doctrine or tooling

Each team receives a case brief describing a company facing maritime sustainability obligations, along with a curated document pack containing EU directives, treaty provisions, and corporate sustainability disclosures.

  1. 1

    Receive case materials

    Case brief (client scenario and scope) and document pack (EU directives, sustainability reports, industry standards).

  2. 2

    Triage and identify provisions

    Review documents for relevant provisions and mark relevant sections for analysis.

  3. 3

    Build obligation register

    Extract requirements from marked sections and link requirements to source passage.

  4. 4

    Construct compliance map

    Show how requirements interact and identify dependencies, conflicts, and overlaps.

  5. 5

    Draft client memorandum

    Assess how obligations apply to the client, with compliance advice and an action plan.

  6. 6

    Reflection on AI use

    Explain verification choices and note where professional judgment was needed.

Guardrail checks throughout

The dashboard requires citations for all claims. References not found are flagged for review, and students verify and revise before proceeding. This makes verification a core learning activity, not just a technical safeguard.

BlueLab is open and platform-independent. Students learn AI-supported compliance as a transferable workflow, enabling graduates to adapt to any corporate platform. The approach can thus be replicated in other settings, courses, and institutions.

Expected outcomes

Impact at three levels

Student-level

Participants will produce specific products, including compliance maps, client-style risk memoranda with action plans, and reflective notes on responsible AI use. These outputs are of direct added value for employability in ESG compliance, sustainability, and corporate responsibility.

Curricular-level

The project delivers a replicable, open-licensed teaching package containing concrete products such as cases, worksheets, rubrics, and a prompt guide. These materials make the innovation scalable to other courses and to programmes in different faculties and universities.

Societal-level

By equipping graduates to translate complex ESG frameworks into actionable compliance advice, BlueLab supports smart governance for the sustainability transition.
